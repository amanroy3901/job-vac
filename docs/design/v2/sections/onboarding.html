Onboarding, sign-in & delivery

From first launch to a proven alert: language, welcome, a plain privacy notice, six quick profile steps, the “you can apply for 7 jobs” payoff, optional Google save, and the permission and phone-setup steps that make sure the last-date alert really arrives.

Rules kept

  • Google Sign-In or guest only. No phone number, OTP, SMS or WhatsApp anywhere
  • Profile and matching stay on the phone (S47 says so first); minor mode under 18
  • One primary pill per screen in the thumb zone; glass action bar over scrolling content
  • Icon beside every label; selection shown by ring + check, never colour alone

Peak–end

Two celebrations: the S10 match count (medallion + marigold confetti) and the S16 “All set” after a real test alert. Failure states never upsell; they offer phone settings and the on-device backup.

New patterns

Step header, selectable tile, radio card, choice chip, DOB boxes, minor-mode card, glass action bar, sheet + dialog, Google button, notification mock, OEM step card, self-test path. See onboarding-NOTES.md.
