States, account, dark, a11y and locales

Everything that is not the happy path: offline, outage, permission off, notices that change under you, account and data rights, minor mode, dark theme, 200% text, TalkBack order, and Tamil and Urdu previews. Same tokens and components as the direction C reference; new pieces are prefixed st-.

  • One banner at a time on Home (outage > offline > notifications off > delivery > trial > profile)
  • Profile and matching live on the phone, so offline and outage states still show correct matches; copy says so
  • Destructive actions are terracotta text or outline, never the filled green button
  • No OTP anywhere: guests type a word, signed-in users confirm with Google
  • Minor mode (under 18): matching and reminders work; account, Pro, ads and telemetry are off and say so

Accessibility and languages

  • 200% text uses Android 14 non-linear scaling (14→28, 16→30, 19→34, 28→47 sp); two-button rows stack, facts stack, chips wrap, nothing truncates
  • Match card = one TalkBack stop that reads the deadline first, then title, verdict, fee and the age rule, with custom actions
  • Tamil: Noto Sans Tamil, line height ×1.05; Urdu: Noto Nastaliq Urdu, ×1.6, mirrored inside the app (status bar not), LTR runs isolated
  • Notice titles stay as published (English/Hindi); the UI around them is translated
