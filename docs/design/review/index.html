Choose a style

Three premium options, shown in English. Each has the same screens: home, end of the feed with the ad, job details, what you need, my forms, the Applied moment with the Pro offer, and dark mode. Every one also works in Hindi.

Option A

Ivory & Ink

Calm and quietly expensive, like a private-bank app. Warm ivory, ink-black buttons, one red accent only when a deadline is close, brass for Pro.

Option B

Midnight Ledger

Dark first, like Cred or Linear. Big countdown numbers read like a departure board, coral for urgency, champagne gold for Pro.

Option C · recommended

Marigold Home

Warm and friendly. Cream, forest-green buttons, marigold highlights, big icons beside every label, and a confetti "Well done, applied!" moment.
