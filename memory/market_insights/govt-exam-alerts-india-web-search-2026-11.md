# Govt-exam alerts (India) — Web Search Trend Analysis

**Data observed as of November 2026.**

Caveats: no direct Google Trends / Keyword Planner access in this run; volumes below are third-party estimates from search-result snippets (Semrush, SEOptimer, Infidigit) and **disagree with each other by up to ~60%**. Trend direction for "sarkari result" could not be established from a primary source — classified **stable** (structural demand; no evidence of a spike or decline; Google's 2025 Year in Search for India did not list it as a breakout, which suggests no spike rather than a decline).

## 1. Executive Summary (Key Insights)

1. **Demand volume is not the problem.** "Sarkari result" is estimated at 79-124M monthly searches in India, ranked #4 among all Indian queries by Infidigit (behind cricbuzz, WhatsApp Web, Satta King).
2. **The intent is informational/navigational, not commercial.** CPC ~$0.01; keyword difficulty 100. People want the *information* (a link to the PDF), not a product.
3. **The SERP is owned.** sarkariresult.com plus dozens of look-alike domains (.com.cm, .com.im, .study...) and edtech blogs (Adda247, Careerpower, Testbook, Oliveboard, Careers360, Jagran Josh) occupy every notification query.
4. **Pull vs push:** search is pull — users come back to check. The idea's bet is that *push* (personalized) beats *pull*. Telegram/WhatsApp channels and edtech app notifications already provide push for free.

## 2. Established Trends

### Head-term navigational cluster: "sarkari result", "sarkari naukri", "free job alert", "sarkari exam"
- **Category:** navigational / informational.
- **Volume:** ~79-124M/mo for "sarkari result" (estimates). Direction: stable.
- **SERP:** aggregator domains; KD 100. CPC ~$0.01.
- **Driver:** structural — ~2-3 crore central-govt applications per year, multi-year prep cycles.

### Exam-event cluster: "<exam> notification 2026", "<exam> admit card", "<exam> answer key", "<exam> result", "<exam> cut off", "<exam> last date"
- **Category:** time-sensitive informational.
- **Pattern:** sharp, short spikes around each board's calendar (SSC, RRB, IBPS, state PSCs). Not annually seasonal; event-seasonal.
- **SERP:** official sites + edtech blogs + aggregators; increasingly AI Overviews for simple date questions.

### Eligibility cluster: "<exam> age limit", "<exam> eligibility", "govt jobs for 12th pass", "govt jobs for graduates", "age limit 30-35 govt jobs"
- **Category:** question-based / problem-driven — closest to the idea's personalization angle.
- **SERP:** TrueJobs (has landing pages like "govt jobs age limit 30-35" and an eligibility checker), Testbook/Adda247 pages.

## 3. Emerging / Rising Trends

- **Eligibility-checker tools** (TrueJobs, Sarkarifilled) — rising as a content format; velocity slow-moderate; volume not measurable here.
- **Official-app queries** ("mySSC app", "SSC OTR") — rising after June 2025 mySSC in-app application launch; moderate velocity.
- **Hindi/vernacular queries** — the head term itself is Hinglish; state-level recruitment queries (police bharti, patwari, TET) in Hindi/regional languages. No quantified growth found (gap).

## 5. Strategic Insights

- SEO for an indie entrant on head terms is futile (KD 100, entrenched aggregators). Long-tail state/vernacular eligibility pages are the only plausible content wedge.
- The commercial intent in this niche lives on **prep** ("mock test") and **assisted application** ("form fill"), not on "notification".
- Push beats search only if the push is *more trustworthy and less noisy* than Telegram — that is an experience/trust gap, not a technology moat.

## Additional Analysis

- **Overhyped vs real:** 100M searches/month looks like a goldmine; CPC $0.01 says the opposite. Aggregator sites survive on enormous page-view volume × tiny ad yields — an indie app would need millions of MAU to make meaningful ad revenue.
- **SERP formats:** list pages with tables of dates and "click here" official links; YouTube videos ("SSC new vacancy 2026") and Telegram links surface prominently.

## 6. Financial Opportunities

1. **Assisted application (transactional)** — Evidence: cyber cafes/CSCs charge INR 30-150 per form (commercial pages; NIOS CSC table ~INR 33). Saturation: fragmented offline, weak online. Sizing (estimate): RRB NTPC + Group D 2025 ≈ 2.3 crore applications; 0.5-1% online-assisted at INR 50 ≈ INR 0.6-1.2 Cr per mega-cycle. Why now: OTR/Aadhaar/face-verification digitization raises friction.
2. **Prep upsell** — Evidence: Testbook ~INR 700/yr, PW INR 399-1,999. Saturated.

## 7. Niche Risks

| Risk | Signal | Severity | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Incumbent SERP moat | KD 100 on head term; aggregator + edtech dominance | High | Avoid head terms; state/vernacular long-tail |
| Query intent mismatch | CPC ~$0.01 despite 100M volume | High | Monetize a transactional job |
| AI search cannibalization | Simple "last date"/"exam date" questions answerable in AI Overviews | Medium | Push-first product |
| Seasonal/event demand cliff | Exam-event spikes; long dead periods per exam | Medium | Multi-exam coverage, prep-side engagement |
| Regulatory/trust | Govt warnings about fake recruitment sites; clone domains | Medium | Official-source linking, transparency |
