# What could make aspirants pay ₹20/month? (2026-11-19)

Principle: free tier = matched alerts (the hook). Paid = "we make sure you don't lose this job because of a mistake or a miss" across the whole application lifecycle. People pay to avoid a known loss: SSC correction fee ₹200/₹500, cyber café ₹100–150/form, a rejected form = a lost year.

## Candidate paid features (ranked by evidence of pain × cost to build)
1. Full lifecycle tracker after "Applied": city slip, admit card, exam date change/reschedule, answer key + objection window, result, cut-off, DV/medical slot ("no further opportunity" if missed). Evidence: SSC DV notices; RRB NTPC rescheduling 2025-26; aspirants miss official comms after moving cities (Adda247 SSC CGL registration problems).
2. Pre-submit form check against the notification: name matches 10th certificate, DOB/age on cutoff with relaxation, category/EWS cert year & format, active mobile/email, photo/signature spec + date stamp. Evidence: TrueJobs rejection guides; Coast Guard rejection list; RRB photo rejections (lakhs).
3. Document vault + auto-resize photo/signature to each exam's spec (on-device). Free elsewhere, so bundle only.
4. Certificate validity & renewal reminders (OBC-NCL/EWS financial year, domicile).
5. Exam clash & reschedule detector across the user's applications; week-preference reminders (e.g. SSC GD week preference deadline).
6. Fee-payment-failed watch ("payment not received" status).
7. Reliable delivery: WhatsApp/SMS fallback when Xiaomi/Vivo/Oppo kill push. Has a real per-message cost, which justifies a fee.
8. Family mode: a parent/sibling also receives the critical reminders.
9. Age-limit / last-attempt countdown per exam.

## Pricing notes
- ₹20/mo = ₹240/yr; Play Billing takes 15% → ~₹204/yr. Offer ₹199/yr (=₹16.6/mo) or ₹49/quarter around application windows; UPI AutoPay is the cheapest rail under ₹499 (Razorpay blog, vendor claim).
- Prior estimate: 0.8% free→paid. To be viable at ₹199/yr you likely need 3–5% conversion plus near-zero CAC. Unproven; must test.

## Test (2 weeks)
Paywall "fake door" in the free alert app or WhatsApp concierge: offer the bundle at ₹199/yr and ₹49 per application season; measure taps and real UPI payments. Pass: ≥3% of active users pay. Kill: <1%.
